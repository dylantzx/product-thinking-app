Lesson 1 · Product Thinking · ~10 min

Problems before solutions

Your mission: build an app that solves a real problem, and know whether it worked. This lesson gives you the first habit.

The idea

The Institute of Digital Government (IDG) puts it plainly: great digital products start with problems, not solutions. A project can be well executed and still struggle if it is solving the wrong problem. The fix is to diagnose the real problem first, then decide what to build.

IDG lists three principles: focus on the problem, chase outcomes, and integrate policy, operations and technology.

Outputs vs outcomes

IDG asks you to define success by the change achieved rather than the features delivered.

Output

Something you deliver: "We launched a booking app."

Outcome

A change in the world: "Fewer people miss appointments."

Your own app can ship every feature and still produce no outcome.

Find the cause: the Five Whys

Start from a visible symptom and ask "why?" repeatedly. IDG notes problems rarely have one root cause, so aim for causes that are within your control and closely connected to the outcome you want to change. Causes too distant or outside your control are hard to act on.

Check yourself

Try it on your app

1. What problem does your app solve, and for whom? 2. Write the outcome as a change ("fewer…", "faster…"). 3. Ask "why?" five times about the problem.
