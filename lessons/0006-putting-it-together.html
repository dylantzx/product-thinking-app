Putting the toolkit together

Five tools, three mindset shifts, and a brief for your own app.

The big picture

The IDG's key takeaways call product thinking a discipline of asking better questions before reaching for solutions. It is a way of working, a set of habits for diagnosing problems, making decisions and learning from what you build, not a method you apply once.

The toolkit: Five Whys, 4Cs, SMART + VCR, staged delivery and the 11-star framework. Three mindset shifts tie it together: from solutions to problems, from outputs to outcomes, and from big-bang to staged delivery.

Mixed review

Questions are mixed across all lessons on purpose. Retrieving from memory is what makes it stick.

Capstone: your one-page product brief

The IDG's next step is to apply the frameworks to one initiative you are already working on, because that is where product thinking becomes real. Assemble your answers from lessons 1 to 5 into one brief for your app.
