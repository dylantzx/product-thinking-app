Write a problem statement with the 4Cs

Lesson 1 found a cause with the Five Whys. Now you say the problem clearly enough that anyone on the team can check it.

The framework

The IDG's 4Cs framework has four dimensions and one statement, with no solutions yet.

Clarity
Who is affected? What are they trying to do? What is broken, and how severely?
Consequence
What happens if we don't solve this? If "not much", it may not be worth solving.
Cause
Why does the problem exist? Connect back to your Five Whys and point toward the root.
Confirmation
What evidence shows the problem is real, significant, and worth prioritising?

The IDG worked example

Repeated form-filling across government services, as shown in the course:

Clarity: citizens fill in the same information 15+ times; each form takes 20–30 minutes; many give up and miss benefits they are entitled to.
Consequence: citizens lose support they qualify for, trust erodes, and the burden falls heaviest on those least equipped to navigate complex processes.
Cause: services were built independently by different agencies at different times, with no shared data layer.
Confirmation: 68% of transactions abandoned due to form-filling frustration (204,000 incomplete out of 300,000 annually).

Notice that nothing says "build a single sign-on form". The statement stops before the solution.

Check yourself

A new example: a clinic where patients skip appointments. Which C is each sentence?

Try it on your app

Draft one sentence or two for each C about your app's problem. Use your five whys from lesson 1 for Cause. If you have no numbers yet, write what evidence you would need to find.
