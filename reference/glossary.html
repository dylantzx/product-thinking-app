Reference

Glossary

Source: IDG Product Thinking.

Product thinking
Defining, scoping and framing a problem before building a solution, so you solve the right problem.
Output
What you deliver, such as features.
Outcome
The change achieved by what you delivered.
Five Whys
Asking "why" repeatedly to move from symptom to actionable cause.
4Cs
Clarity, Consequence, Cause, Confirmation: a framework for a data-backed problem statement. Clarity: who is affected and how badly. Consequence: stakes if unsolved. Cause: root reason. Confirmation: evidence. See lesson 2.
Policy-Ops-Tech
Integrating policy, operations and technology rather than treating tech alone.
SMART
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound: five properties of a good metric.
Leading indicator
Easy to measure, gives early feedback.
Lagging indicator
Closer to the outcome, verifies real impact.
Value-Cost Ratio (VCR)
Units of value generated per dollar spent. One input among several, since not all public value is in dollars.
Market / technical / team risk
Will anyone use it and does it solve the problem; is it too complex or costly to build; do we have the right capabilities.
Four stages of de-risking
Proof of concept, proof of value, scale, maturity.
11-star framework
Imagine experiences from 1 to 11 stars to push past good enough, then work back to what is achievable.
