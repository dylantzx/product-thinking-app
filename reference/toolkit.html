Toolkit cheat sheet

Source: IDG Product Thinking. Order of use: problem, cause, statement, metric, risk, experience.

1 Five Whys lesson 1

Ask why repeatedly. Pick causes you control that are closely tied to the outcome.

2 4Cs lesson 2

Clarity who and how bad · Consequence stakes if unsolved · Cause root reason · Confirmation evidence. No solutions.

3 Metrics lesson 3

SMART check. Chain leading to lagging indicators. VCR = value ÷ cost.

4 Staged delivery lesson 4

Market, technical, team risk. Proof of concept, proof of value, scale, maturity. Run the simplest experiment on the key risk.

5 11-star lesson 5

Map 1 to 11 stars, then choose what is ambitious and achievable. Fix specific frustrations; raise the bar when use is mandatory.

Mindset shifts

Solutions to problems · outputs to outcomes · big-bang to staged delivery.
