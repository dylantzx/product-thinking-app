# Mission: Product Thinking

## Why
Build my own AI/digital app (this workspace's product-thinking-app) so that it solves a real problem and I can tell whether it worked, instead of just shipping features.

## Success looks like
- Write a clear, data-backed problem statement for my app before building
- Tell outputs from outcomes and pick a metric that measures the outcome
- Run a Five Whys on a symptom and choose a root cause I can act on
- De-risk the app in stages instead of one big launch

## Constraints
- Total beginner; short lessons, based on the Institute of Digital Government (IDG) Product Thinking course

## Out of scope
- Formal product-management career topics (roadmaps, org design)
